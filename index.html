WHAT IS ALTIMA ML VISION?

A personal AI Sports Assistant that stays with the user before, during and after every workout.

The full Altima ML Vision experience is designed around AR/VR glasses. The virtual trainer stays in the user's field of view, while cameras and wearables help AI analyze movement and deliver immediate guidance.
