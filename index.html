WHAT IS ALTIMA ML VISION?

A personal AI Sports Assistant that stays with the user before, during and after every workout.

The core experience works with accessible hardware. A camera observes movement, AI compares it with the target technique, and the user receives immediate guidance.

◎

See the movement

✓

↻

✦

Demonstrate technique

A virtual trainer can show the correct movement and explain what should change.
